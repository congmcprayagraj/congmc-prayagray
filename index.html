ABOUT THE COLLEGE

Developing the nurses healthcare needs tomorrow.

College of Nursing, Government Medical College Prayagraj was established in 2021 and offers B.Sc. Nursing education in a professional government medical college environment.

The college focuses on strong theoretical foundations, practical competence, professional discipline, ethical practice and clinical exposure through the associated government hospital environment.

ADMISSIONS

B.Sc. Nursing admission

Official ABVMU ↗

How to apply

Admission is through the UP Common Nursing Entrance Test (CNET) and subsequent counselling as notified by ABVMU.

Eligibility

  • Minimum age: 17 years by 31 December of the admission year.
  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English from a recognized board.
  • 45% PCB aggregate for General category; 40% for SC/ST/OBC as provided.
  • UP domicile / qualifying UP education requirement for government seats, subject to current counselling rules.

Documents

  • CNET admit card & rank/result card
  • Seat allotment letter & fee receipts
  • TC / Migration Certificate
  • Character Certificate
  • UP Domicile Certificate
  • Category Certificate, if applicable
  • Medical Fitness Certificate
  • 5–6 passport-size photographs

Fees & dates

Fee structure is subject to applicable government guidelines/criteria. Important dates follow the official counselling schedule.
